# Revival

People you've already spoken to know who you are, and they told you on the call what they needed and why it wasn't the right time. Old calls beat bought lists. This skill finds the ones worth another go and drafts the follow-up from their own words.

## 1. What you need

- **Calls:** the call recorder, through whatever is connected (Fathom, Fireflies, Gong, Zoom, Google Meet and so on), or transcripts the owner exported into a folder. If neither is available, ask for an export.
- **`business.md`:** the offer, the ICP, who to skip, objections and answers, approved claims and proof, voice and sign-off.
- **`meetings/meetings.md`** and the CRM if connected: what happened after each call.
- **The do-not-contact list.**

Ask the owner which period to cover, for example calls from the last 6 to 12 months.

## 2. Pick the calls

Leave out:

- current customers and won deals
- anyone on the do-not-contact list, or who asked not to be contacted
- anyone already in an active campaign or a live conversation
- internal calls, partner calls, interviews, and anything that isn't a sales call
- calls with no transcript or summary. List these for the owner instead of guessing what was said

## 3. Read each call

From the transcript or summary, take:

- who was on the call, their role and company
- what they wanted to fix, in their words
- why it didn't go ahead, in their words: timing, budget, someone else decides, went with someone else, went quiet
- any timing they gave ("look again after the summer", "next financial year")
- objections raised, and what was promised as a next step

Copy the exact lines you rely on, with the call date and the timestamp if there is one. Don't copy whole transcripts into the workspace: they hold private conversation. Keep only the quotes you need.

## 4. Score it

Give each call two scores, each with a one-line reason and the quote behind it.

| Score | Values | Based on |
|---|---|---|
| ICP fit | fit, not a fit, unsure | `business.md` ICP and who to skip, plus the company's site as it is now |
| Warmth | hot, warm, cold | Hot: they wanted it and gave a reason or time to come back that has now passed. Warm: real interest, but the blocker may still be there. Cold: not interested, or no clear need |

Draft emails only for fit + hot and fit + warm. List the rest with their reasons so the owner can overrule you.

## 5. Draft the follow-up

Each draft:

- refers to the call and one specific thing they said. Paraphrase it, or quote it exactly if you use quote marks
- says why now, using only real reasons: the timing they gave has arrived, something they were waiting on was mentioned, or something new in `business.md` (an offer, proof or feature) answers their blocker
- ends with one low-friction ask, or one specific time from the booking skill
- is short, plain, in the owner's voice and sign-off, with only approved claims

Never:

- invent a quote, a detail from the call, or a reason to reach out
- claim to remember something that isn't in the transcript
- pretend a mass email is personal: every draft must use something from that call

**Which mailbox:** by default, the owner's own mailbox they used with this person before, not a cold-sending inbox. These are people the owner has spoken to. Use another mailbox only if the owner says so.

## 6. Save and hand over

Save everything in `campaigns/revival-YYYY-MM/`:

- `calls.md`: one row per call with date, person, company, ICP fit, warmth, reason, the key quote, and draft yes or no
- `drafts.md`: each draft with the call it came from and the quote it relies on

Show the owner the table and the drafts. Every revival email stays a draft until the owner approves it, even if other kinds of email are allowed to go out automatically. When one is sent, note it in `calls.md`, and if they book, the booking skill takes it from there.

## 7. Self-check

- [ ] Customers, do-not-contact, active conversations and non-sales calls were left out.
- [ ] Every score has a reason and a quote with the call date.
- [ ] Every draft uses something real from that call, and nothing invented.
- [ ] Quotes inside quote marks are exact.
- [ ] Only approved claims; the "why now" is a real reason.
- [ ] Drafts use the owner's mailbox unless they said otherwise.
- [ ] No full transcripts saved, only the quotes needed.
- [ ] Nothing was sent without the owner's approval.
